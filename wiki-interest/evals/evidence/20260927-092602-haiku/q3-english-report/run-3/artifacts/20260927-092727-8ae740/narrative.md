# Which audiences should we research next for a language-learning app?

## Bottom line

German (23,694 views/month) and Turkish (7,046 views/month) show stable interest in English, while Polish, Czech, and Ukrainian are declining. German is your priority expansion market; Turkish represents an underexploited opportunity. Investigate why Polish, Czech, and Ukrainian are declining—this may reveal market saturation or infrastructure gaps.

## Next

- **Research German market first**: largest stable audience, no competing learning resources apparent on Wikipedia trends; validate product-market fit locally before scaling to other European languages.
- **Validate Turkish opportunity**: flat trend with good-sized audience suggests untapped demand; cheaper localization than declining markets due to no need to reverse downward trends.
- **Investigate Polish/Czech decline**: 9.8–10.9% drops suggest either saturation or specific friction; interview users to identify whether existing solutions are adequate or if gaps exist that your app can fill.
- **Monitor Ukrainian separately**: 13.9% decline is steeper; geopolitical context may drive lower interest; validate that content and marketing resonate with regional challenges and opportunities.
