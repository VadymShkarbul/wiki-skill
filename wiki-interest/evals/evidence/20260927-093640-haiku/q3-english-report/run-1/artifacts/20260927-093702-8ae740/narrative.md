# Which markets show interest in learning English?

## Bottom line

Interest in the English language is stagnant or declining across Eastern European Wikipedias (Polish, Czech, Ukrainian), but stable in German and Turkish. German is the largest audience (23,694 views/month), while Turkish (7,046 views/month) is the only other market showing no clear trend. German should be your next research target, followed by Turkish to validate willingness to pay in a non-Western European market.

## Next

- **Research the German market first**: it has 3× the viewership of Polish, no declining trend, and sits between Western and Eastern Europe geographically. Validate product-market fit and monetization appetite.
- **Validate Turkish interest independently**: Wikipedia views are flat, but Turkish learners may engage differently. Survey or interview users; it's a large, growing economy not yet well-studied.
- **Revisit Eastern Europe later**: declines may reflect shifting to YouTube or apps, not lost demand. Re-approach once your app gains traction.
- **Monitor whether Wikipedia declines track app demand**: page view declines in these markets could signal that learners have moved to more specialized tools. Confirm before deprioritizing.
