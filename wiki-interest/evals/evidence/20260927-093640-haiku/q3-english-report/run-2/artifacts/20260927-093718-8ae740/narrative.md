# Which audiences should we research next for English language learning?

## Bottom line

German and Turkish Wikipedia readers show stable interest in English, while Polish, Czech, and Ukrainian readers show clear declining interest. German is the most promising lead: it has the largest audience (23,694 views/month) with no decline, and Turkish offers a second opportunity with 7,046 views/month and similarly flat trajectory. Investigate what's working in those markets while the Eastern European markets shift strategy.

## Next

- **German market first**: largest audience (23,694 views/month), stable interest, established market. Validate why German sustains engagement as others decline.
- **Turkish opportunity second**: stable 7,046 views/month in untapped market. Test regional positioning and mobile-first strategies.
- **Investigate Eastern European decline**: Polish (-9.8%), Czech (-10.9%), Ukrainian (-13.9%) dropping consistently. Diagnose causes—competitors, free alternatives, or shifts in demand.
- **Segment Polish engagement**: still 7,913 views/month, so identify which learner segments remain active versus those converting to competitors.
