# Which English-learning audiences should we research next?

## Bottom line
German and Turkish audiences show stable interest in English (no clear trend), while Polish, Czech, and Ukrainian show declining attention. German offers the largest and most stable audience (23,694 views/month), making it the strongest candidate for initial market research. Turkish, though smaller (7,046 views/month), presents a stable second-tier opportunity with potentially less competition.

## Next
- **Research German first**: Largest stable audience (23,694 views/month) with high market potential; assess competitive landscape early.
- **Explore Turkish as secondary market**: Stable interest (7,046 views/month) with potentially less competition than German-speaking regions.
- **Investigate Eastern European decline**: Polish, Czech, and Ukrainian show -9.8% to -13.9% drops—understand if this reflects saturation or migration to other learning platforms.
- **Validate Wikipedia trends with market data**: Cross-check Wikipedia signals against app downloads, course enrollments, and user surveys in each region.
