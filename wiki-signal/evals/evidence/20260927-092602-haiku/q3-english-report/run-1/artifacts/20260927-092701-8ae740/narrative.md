# Which language-learning markets should we research next?

## Bottom line
German (23,694 views/month) and Turkish (7,046 views/month) show stable interest, while Polish, Czech, and Ukrainian all decline with high confidence. Research German first—your largest audience with no trend decline. Then validate Turkish as a stable, potentially untapped market.

## Next
- **Prioritize German**: 3x larger than Turkish, showing flat interest. Study how competitors serve German learners and what features drive engagement.
- **Validate Turkish**: Stable 7,046 monthly views. Check whether competitors have thin coverage in this market.
- **Investigate Slavic decline**: Polish (-9.8%), Czech (-10.9%), Ukrainian (-13.9%) all falling consistently. Determine if learners shifted to apps or other platforms before investing.
- **Monitor scale**: Czech is small (2,947 views/month). Prioritize German and Turkish volume over smaller markets.
